# The Annual Dinner

The Computer Science Annual Dinner runs during Easter term every year. Students, fellows, supervisors, and alumni are all invited.

The evening usually begins with a reception in the Old Kitchens, followed by a 3-course dinner in the Fitzpatrick Hall, and finishing in the Old Hall with more food and drink. Additionally, an industry speaker is invited each year to give a tech talk.
