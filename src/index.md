# Welcome to the Queens' Computer Science Society!

We are the society for all computer science students of Queens' College at the University of Cambridge.

## About us

Our main meetings are every Wednesday evening during term. These vary a lot, with common activities including:
- Tech talks from industry speakers
- Student vacation presentations
- Dissertation presentations
- Open discussions
- Game nights
- The annual \*\*\*\*\*\*

The society also organises other events throughout the year:
- Mahal
- The [Annual Dinner](/dinner/)
- Post-exam punting

## The Robin Walker Fund
Thanks to the Robin Walker Fund, members of the society can be reimbursed for any computer science related personal projects. This is a subject fund maintained by the college. Further information can be found in the [subject fund section of the college website](https://www.queens.cam.ac.uk/teaching-and-learning/tutorial-office/student-administration/financial-support-for-current-students/#section-2).
